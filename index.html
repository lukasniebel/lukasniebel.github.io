Welcome!

I am a mathematician working in analysis and partial differential equations. My research interests include kinetic and hypoelliptic equations, nonlinear diffusion, and free-boundary problems in fluid mechanics. More broadly, I am interested in the interplay between transport, diffusion, geometry, and regularity in nonlinear PDEs describing complex phenomena in nature.
